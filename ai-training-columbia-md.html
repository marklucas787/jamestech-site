The morning

How the four hours run.

  • →8:30. The teammate-or-stranger demo, live, on the instructor's own system. Ask any AI a question about your business and hear the difference. Then: by noon, yours does this.
  • →8:45. Round the room. Each owner names the one workflow they brought.
  • →9:00. Install. Claude on every machine, Windows and Mac, with the instructor walking the room.
  • →9:45. Business context. A structured interview turns into a context file the AI reads every session. This is the step that makes it yours.
  • →10:40. Connect the business. Documents in, first real answers out. Ask it something only your business would know.
  • →11:15. The daily brief. Each attendee stands up their first standing job.
  • →11:50. Automate the workflow you brought, or scope it live if it is too big for the hour.
  • →12:15. What is next, and questions. Hallway conversations after.
