The definition

What a fractional AI officer is.

A fractional AI officer is a senior person who owns your company's AI strategy, governance, and rollout on a part-time retainer instead of a full-time salary. The role is also called a fractional Chief AI Officer, or fractional CAIO. Same job, different title.

For a 5 to 50 person firm the math is simple. There is real AI work to do every month: which automations to build next, which vendors to trust, what the team is allowed to do with client data, whether last quarter's investment paid off. There is not forty hours a week of it. A fractional officer fits the actual size of the work.

JamesTech LLC provides Fractional AI Officer retainers to firms in and around Columbia, Maryland, and remotely across the Baltimore-Washington corridor. Retainers start at $3,700 a month.

The work

What you get each month.

  • →Automations shipped. One to two new automations or AI recipes a month, built on Microsoft 365, Power Automate, Power Apps, Dataverse, and SharePoint, inside your tenant.
  • →Roadmap ownership. Someone accountable for what gets built next and why, including vendor evaluation, an AI usage policy the team can follow, and governance for client data.
  • →Training. A monthly session with your team on the real work, so the firm keeps the capability rather than renting it.
  • →Reporting. A monthly plain-English status report, and each quarter an ROI report for leadership that shows hours and dollars returned against the retainer.
  • →Security and compliance in view. Data handling, access, and regulatory constraints documented and flagged on every recommendation. Mark has handled Controlled Unclassified Information for NASA and delivered for the DOJ and NATO.
