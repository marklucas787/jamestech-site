Questions

The honest answers.

What exactly is a "skill"? +

A repeatable workflow James executes on one instruction: build my morning brief, prep me for this meeting, draft the weekly status report, process this transcript into action items. My own system runs more than 80 of them. Your install ships with the ones mapped to your business in the Review, and new ones get added as we find the work.

Where does our data live? +

In your own tenant and your own accounts, under permissions you control. James works through the Microsoft 365, accounting, and CRM subscriptions you already hold. Nothing is uploaded to my servers. For regulated environments, we map your data and compliance constraints before anything is built.

Can it send an email or an invoice without us knowing? +

No. Outbound is draft-only by standing rule. James prepares the email, the report, or the invoice and parks it for your approval. You are the signature on everything that leaves the building.

Do we have to be on Microsoft? +

It's where I add the most value and where most of my clients already are, but the system connects to accounting, CRM, calendar, and communication tools broadly. If you're partly on Microsoft, we'll still find the wins.

What happens when the big AI companies build this themselves? +

The tools underneath will keep improving, and your system improves with them. What you're buying from me is the part that doesn't commoditize: your business context, your governance rules, your workflows mapped and automated, and a senior advisor who is accountable for the roadmap. When a better engine ships, we swap it in and you get faster.

Who is this for? +

Owner-led professional services firms, roughly 10 to 50 people, where the owner is the bottleneck and the data can't leave the building. If that's you, the Review is the right first step. If you're a solo operator who wants to build this yourself, I'm probably not your vendor, and I'll tell you that on the call.
